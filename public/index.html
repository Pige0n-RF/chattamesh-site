Our Mission

Community-Owned Communication for Chattanooga

ChattaMesh exists to give this area a way to talk to each other that no company, cell tower, or outage controls. Here's the idea, piece by piece.

What MeshCore Is

MeshCore is open-source firmware that turns inexpensive LoRa radios into a self-healing mesh. Each device relays messages for its neighbors, carrying them hop by hop, well past the range of any single radio — no cell towers, no Wi-Fi, no monthly bill.

A Decentralized Network

There's no central server to knock offline and no single company that owns it. Every node — handheld or repeater — is an equal participant, so the network keeps working even if any one piece goes down.

Built in the Open

The firmware, the hardware designs, and this site are all open source. Anyone can inspect how it works, suggest a change, or fork it outright. The network belongs to the people who build and maintain it — not a company.

Organizing Locally

ChattaMesh coordinates the Chattanooga-area chapter of this effort — agreeing on shared settings, regions, and channels so local nodes can actually hear each other — in partnership with the wider TennMesh network.

Grown by Volunteers

Every repeater on a ridge or rooftop was placed by someone who cared enough to put it there. Coverage grows only as fast as volunteers extend it — mount a repeater, join a channel, or just help spread the word.
