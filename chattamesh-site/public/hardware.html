DIY Supplies

Parts for building your own repeater.

Enclosures

PVC — screw cover

Cheapest weatherproof option. Screw cover means four screws every time you need to get in, so pick this when you expect to set it and forget it.

Search “PVC junction box screw cover NEMA 4X 6x6x4” — Cantex and Carlon are the usual brands, in any big-box electrical aisle.

PVC — hinge cover

Same enclosure, hinged lid. Worth the small upcharge on anything you'll be opening to swap batteries or reflash. Ships with a mounting plate, brackets and cable glands.

Buy Now

Aluminum — hinge cover

Step up for highsite and infrastructure builds. Rigid, UV-proof, and won't go chalky after a few Tennessee summers. Remember the metal is an RF barrier — the antenna has to be outside it.

Search “cast aluminum enclosure hinged IP67”

Aluminum — Nebra

Nebra-style extruded aluminum case with end plates. Popular for the style, the threaded holes, and the fact that the enclosure itself is a heatsink to keep those batteries cool!

Buy Now

RAK3401 core + RAK13302 booster + RAK19007 base. Better for residential roof repeaters — “whole side of town” coverage, with built-in filters. Formerly sold as the “1W”.

Buy Now

Heltec V3

The V4's predecessor and still everywhere. Same footprint, cheaper, and the one most guides are written against.

Buy Now

Heltec V4

ESP32 board, same footprint and pinout as the V3. Cheap, easy to find and well supported. Draws far more at idle than the RAK boards, so it suits a mains-powered node better than a solar repeater.

Batteries

Only buy cells from a trusted source. We're deliberately not linking Amazon for cells — counterfeit and rewrapped ones are rampant, and a fake cell in a sealed box on someone's roof is the one failure mode in this whole page that starts a fire. Try 18650batterystore.com, illumn.com, or liionwholesale.com.

Li-ion 18650

CellsSamsung 30Q or 35E, Panasonic NCR18650GA

Standard option. Solid.

Li-ion 21700

CellsSamsung 40T, Molicel P42A

Upgraded option. Very solid, higher capacity.

Li-ion pouch cell

Spicy pillows. We would advise against those.

Solar managers

Some people open the backing on a panel and pull the voltage regulator out. Without it the panel puts out around 16 V. Check what your board or solar manager can actually accept before you do that — the regulator is the only thing standing between a bright day and a dead board.

Waveshare Solar Power Manager

Takes the panel in, charges the pack, and hands your board a regulated 5 V. Screw terminals for the panel and battery, USB-C and a barrel jack, and a four-LED charge gauge.
